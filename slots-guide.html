What is exclusive?

  • Pretty Penny: spreading Penny Wilds and Reveal Doors. Spreading wilds replace two to four regular winning tiles while preserving every resolved line award. Reveal Doors hide selected rows or individual symbols until the reveal; they change presentation only.
  • Treasure Pots: three to five SCATTER chest symbols appear when its configured free-spin trigger succeeds. The chests show the award; they do not add a second trigger roll.
  • Neon Slots: Hot Reels randomly ignites one reel and holds a sticky Wild for five spins, with a configurable minimum return initially set to 2× wager per active spin. The guarantee increases effective returns beyond regular Win Odds. Reveal winnings covers the reels with immediate results, automatically resolves bonuses under their existing rules, and uses the same counted auto-spin runs as all four slots. Controls and Settings remain accessible; odds and credit accounting stay the same.
  • Big Money Deluxe: currently has no exclusive bonus mechanic among these four games.

Does Pretty Penny have SCATTER chests?

No. Pretty Penny has free spins, but awards them without the SCATTER chest display. All four slots have a configurable free-spin chance and retain the triggering wager for those spins.

Auto-spin counts in every slot

Use default auto-spin count is enabled initially with 25 spins, saved separately for each game. Leave it enabled to start without a prompt, or turn it off to choose 1–10,000 spins before each run. Every completed paid or free spin uses one turn. Normal wagers apply to paid spins; this setting does not award free spins. Cancel or insufficient credits can stop a run early.

Shared features with different themes

Pretty Penny’s savings banks and Treasure Pots’ pot meters share the same collecting mechanic: connected values advance their matching meter, which pays when full. Their sticky LINK and held-wild features are also shared mechanics with different artwork and names.

All four offer configurable jackpots, wheel pointers, spin history, and automatic affordable bets. A shared feature does not mean identical prizes or odds. Consult each game’s Payouts & rules for its paytable and current settings.
