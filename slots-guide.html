What is exclusive?

  • Pretty Penny: Growing Penny Wild, spreading Penny Wilds, and Reveal Doors. Growing Penny Wild expands a natural wild across all five cells of one eligible row for the current paid or free spin. Its saved trigger chance defaults to 3% and can be disabled. It can add payline wins, preserves existing line awards and special symbols, skips jackpot displays, and increases returns beyond Regular Win Odds. Spreading wilds replace two to four regular winning tiles while preserving every resolved line award. Reveal Doors hide selected rows or individual symbols until the reveal; they change presentation only.
  • Treasure Pots: three to five SCATTER chest symbols appear when its configured free-spin trigger succeeds. The chests show the award; they do not add a second trigger roll.
  • Neon Slots: Hot Reels randomly ignites one reel and holds a sticky Wild for five spins, with a configurable minimum return initially set to 2× wager per active spin. The guarantee increases effective returns beyond regular Win Odds.
  • Big Money Deluxe: currently has no exclusive bonus mechanic among these four games.

Are spreading wilds and SCATTER symbols the same?

No. Pretty Penny’s spreading wilds replace a few regular winning tiles while preserving resolved awards. Its Growing Penny Wild fills an eligible row and can add payline winnings. Treasure Pots’ SCATTER chests display free-spin awards and do not substitute as wilds. Neon Slots’ Hot Reels holds a single sticky wild on a fiery reel, rather than filling a row.

Does Pretty Penny have SCATTER chests?

No. Pretty Penny has free spins, but awards them without the SCATTER chest display. All four slots have a configurable free-spin chance and retain the triggering wager for those spins.

Auto-spin counts in every slot

Use default auto-spin count is enabled initially with 25 spins, saved separately for each game. Leave it enabled to start without a prompt, or turn it off to choose 1–10,000 spins before each run. Every completed paid or free spin uses one turn. Normal wagers apply to paid spins; this setting does not award free spins. Cancel or insufficient credits can stop a run early.

Phone controls and win messages

Controls fills the game area on phones. Scroll to reach settings and wagers, and use Done to return to the reels. Long win messages scroll with the reel area. Settings descriptions are centered with space on both sides.

Rapid spins and Reveal winnings

Press Spin to start the reels, then Stop to show that result early. Once it settles, press Spin again. Stop only shortens the animation. In every slot, enable Reveal winnings in Settings to see the wager, award, net result, and balance immediately. Bonuses resolve automatically under their usual rules. Reveal winnings also works during counted auto-spin runs.

Phone play in every slot

All four slots scale reel icons and award labels to fit their cells. Award values retain a minimum 12-pixel font on narrow phones. On very short screens, scroll inside the reel area to see every row and the result; the bottom play controls stay visible.

Shared features with different themes

Pretty Penny’s savings banks and Treasure Pots’ pot meters share the same collecting mechanic: connected values advance their matching meter, which pays when full. Their sticky LINK and held-wild features are also shared mechanics with different artwork and names.

All four offer configurable jackpots, wheel pointers, spin history, and automatic affordable bets. A shared feature does not mean identical prizes or odds. Consult each game’s Payouts & rules for its paytable and current settings.
