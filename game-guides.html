  1. Choose Denom, active Lines, and a Total Bet button. On phones, open Controls to reach these settings.

Monopoly Deal v1.6.0

Low hands refill to at least five cards when drawing at the start of a turn or with Pass Go, drawing the usual two when that already reaches five. Empty hands draw five, and seven remains the end-of-turn limit. Automatic dealing adds the cards immediately; manual dealing requires one deck tap per owed card. This minimum-five refill is a house rule; standard rules draw two unless the hand is empty.

Neon Slots v1.5

Choose denomination, active lines, and Total Bet, then Spin. Phone Controls fills the game screen. Scroll for wagers and settings, and use Done to return to play. All four slots share counted auto spins, automatic affordable bets, free spins, the three bonus games, and spin history. Use Payouts & rules for current paytables and settings.

Pretty Penny v1.5

Choose denomination, lines, and Total Bet, then Spin. Open the full-screen Controls panel on phones for wagers and settings. Payouts & rules contains the full paytable and feature descriptions.

  • Growing Penny Wild: a natural wild can grow across all five cells of one eligible row for the current paid or free spin. The saved trigger defaults to 3% and can be changed or disabled. Active paylines pay from the expanded grid. Existing line awards never decrease, special-symbol rows are protected, and jackpot displays skip growth. This can increase wins beyond Regular Win Odds.
  • Spreading Penny Wilds: replace two to four regular winning tiles while preserving resolved line awards. Growing and spreading are separate features.
  • Reveal Doors: hide selected rows or symbols until reveal; this changes presentation only.
  • Shared with TreasurePots: LINK values, collecting meters, sticky first-reel LINK anchors with fresh values, and three-spin held wild gems. LINK chips are not wilds. Sticky LINK and held gems use separate rows.

Growing wilds, spreading wilds, and Reveal Doors are exclusive to Pretty Penny. It has free spins but does not display Treasure Pots’ SCATTER chests. Counted auto spins default to 25, and Automatically lower bet starts enabled.

Sorry! v1.1

  1. Two local players have four pawns each. The first player to bring all four pawns Home wins.
  2. Press Draw and choose one of the legal action buttons. This implementation lets a pawn leave Start on any legal number card; a SORRY! card can replace an opponent’s pawn on the main track with one of yours from Start.
  3. Most cards move forward. A 4 moves backward four; a 10 offers forward ten or backward one. An 11 offers forward eleven or a swap between pawns on the main track. A 7 can move one pawn or split its seven steps between two eligible pawns.
  4. Landing on an opponent bumps that pawn to Start. Landing at an opponent-color slide can carry your pawn along it and bump opponents in its path. Your own occupied destination blocks a move.
  5. Enter Home by exact count. Use the Game Log to review completed moves and follow the Turn indicator for the next player.

The action buttons show the moves supported by the current card and board. They are the source of available choices for this classic-style implementation.

TreasurePots v1.5

Choose denomination, active lines, and Total Bet, then Spin. On phones, Controls contains wagers and settings. Read Payouts & rules for the current paytable, jackpots, and configurable feature chances.

  • SCATTER chests: three to five appear when the configured free-spin trigger succeeds. They show the free-spin award; they are not substituting wilds and do not add another trigger roll.
  • LINK and pot meters: connected values pay and can advance their matching pot meter on paid spins. A non-paying Not Connected chain is a preview, not a win.
  • Sticky LINK Row: holds a first-reel anchor for five reel spins by default while connected values refresh. It can use active free spins but starts on paid spins. The anchor is a LINK, not a wild.
  • Emerald Hold & Win: valued wild gems accumulate in a separate row for three spins. A full five-cell row awards its combined values; an unfinished row clears after the third spin.

LINK, meters, sticky anchors, held gems, and bonus-wheel jackpots are shared with Pretty Penny. Growing and spreading Penny Wilds are unavailable here. Shared counted auto spins, automatic affordable bets, bonus games, and spin history remain available.

Press Spin, then Stop to show that spin’s result early. Enable Reveal winnings in Settings to see results immediately and resolve bonuses automatically.

Yahtzee v1.1

  1. Play thirteen rounds on one scorecard. Each round allows up to three rolls of five dice.
  2. Press Roll, then tap dice to hold or release them before rolling again. You may score after the first roll; using all three is optional.
  3. Choose an unused scorecard category. The displayed preview becomes its permanent score, including zero when the dice do not qualify. Each category can be used once.
  4. Ones through Sixes total only the matching dice. Reaching an upper subtotal of 63 awards a 35-point bonus. Three/Four of a Kind score the dice total when eligible. Full House scores 25, Small Straight 30, Large Straight 40, and Yahtzee 50. Chance always scores the total.
  5. After thirteen categories, review the final total and use New Game to start a fresh scorecard. Scroll the scorecard to reach every category on a small screen.

This implementation scores a full house as exactly a pair plus a three-of-a-kind. It does not add a repeat-Yahtzee bonus or joker scoring.
