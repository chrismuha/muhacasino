Payout Rules

Regular symbol payout = paytable multiplier × bet per line (credits) × denomination. LINK values, savings banks, and jackpots use their displayed dollar amounts instead.

Only matching regular symbols from the left count. Three, four, or five in a row on an active payline pays.

The TreasurePots wild substitutes for any regular symbol from the left. All-wild hits pay the wild row.

Reel jackpots use a shared event independent from regular Win Odds. The highest selected Jackpot Odds value sets the chance that at least one reel jackpot occurs, while each tier retains its selected chance. Multiple reel jackpots and a regular line win can occur together. When every tier is set to 1%, the overall reel-jackpot chance is 1% per spin.

On rare non-jackpot spins, one or two unlit jackpot chips may appear as near misses. These display-only chips do not award a jackpot or change the configured jackpot odds.

Penny Emerald awards scale upward with the total bet. At total bets up to $0.50, the guaranteed minimums are Mini $250, Minor $1,000, Major $5,000, and Grand $25,000. Above $0.50, every tier increases in direct proportion to the total bet. The Mini always remains greater than the $100 Royal Pot sub-jackpot.

Free Spins and Emerald Wheel

Free Spins and Emerald Wheel settings are loading.

Free spins cost no credits and retain the denomination, active lines, and bet per line from the paid spin that awarded them. Their payouts are added to Available Credits. Free spins cannot retrigger either feature.

The TreasurePots wheel activates only when three, four, or five Emerald Wheel chips land from the left on an active payline. The trigger chips are labeled “Emerald / Spin Wheel.” Regular coin outcomes are Copper Coin at 1× the triggering bet (35%), Lucky Coin at 2× (25%), Ruby Coin at 5× (18%), and Golden Coin at 10× (12%). Penny Emerald jackpots are much rarer: Mini 7%, Minor 2%, Major 0.8%, and Grand 0.2%. Jackpot dollar values scale with the triggering bet. Wheel odds are fixed and separate from the reel Jackpot Odds settings.

Savings Banks & Penny Chains

Only a winning LINK chain on a paid spin can attempt to advance the savings banks. On each qualifying LINK win, Pocket Pot has a 72% chance to gain 2–6%, Lucky Pot has a 48% chance to gain 2–6%, and Royal Pot has a 28% chance to gain 2–6%. Their fixed awards are $5, $25, and $100. A bank opens only after its own meter reaches 100%, then resets to 0%. Unfinished banks keep their progress. Free-spin LINK wins do not advance the banks.

Six or more gold value coins trigger Hold & Link. The coins lock in place and one guaranteed COLLECT coin gathers every displayed dollar value into a single Big Money-style total. Two to five coins may appear as a non-paying teaser.

Settings Guide

Session Stats Display: Chooses which stat boxes are visible (regular session totals, Net totals, or Actual Net totals).

Winnings Only / Losses Only: Shows only regular session winnings or regular session losses.

Both Net / Net Winnings Only / Net Losses Only: Uses your running net-style counters where wins and losses are tracked as separate buckets.

Both Actual Net / Actual Net Winnings Only / Actual Net Losses Only: Uses true net math from one value (wins minus bets), then displays positive side as winnings and negative side as losses.

Session Winnings: Total amount won this session (sum of all payouts).

Session Losses: Total loss this session from spins where bet exceeded payout.

Net Session Winnings / Net Session Losses: Legacy net-style totals where each side can stay positive at the same time.

Actual Net Session Winnings / Actual Net Session Losses: True net totals where only one side is non-zero at a time.

Denomination (Value Per Credit): Dollar value of one credit. Higher denomination increases bet size and payout size proportionally.

Lines: Number of active paylines (1-10). More lines means more chances to win, but higher total bet.

Bet Per Line: Credits wagered on each active line. Increasing this raises both total bet and possible payout.

Win Odds: Sets the target chance that a spin resolves as a win. Winning payout amounts still come from the paytable.

Free Spins Win / Loss Ratio: Sets the independent chance that a paid spin awards free spins.

Number of Free Spins Awarded: Sets how many free games are added when the feature triggers.

Emerald Wheel Trigger Win / Loss Ratio: Sets the independent chance that a paid spin lands a winning line of Emerald Wheel chips and opens the TreasurePots wheel. It does not alter the fixed 90% regular-coin / 10% jackpot outcome mix.

Winning LINK Chain Win / Loss Ratio: Sets the chance that an eligible winning spin replaces part of a reel row with a first-reel LINK and connected value chips.

Not Connected LINK Display Ratio: Sets the chance that a spin without a winning LINK displays a colorful, non-paying “Not Connected” LINK chain.

Jackpot Odds: Controls jackpots that appear directly on the reels only. Each tier offers presets shown as “1 in N spins,” plus Custom percentage controls. These settings do not alter the bonus wheel. Jackpot dollar values scale with total bet using $0.50 as the base wager, but never fall below the displayed minimums. Custom odds clear on Reset Session or page reload.

Settings: Opens the paginated settings dialog. Use Show this setting on the home screen to pin individual settings. Denomination is pinned by default.

Adjust Credit + ▲/▼: Sets how much credit to add/remove per click, scaled by denomination.

Skip Winning Highlight Delay: Removes the auto-spin pause after winning reels are highlighted.

Spin: Plays one round using current settings and deducts the total bet first. On desktop, tap Spacebar for one spin or hold it for auto spin; Spacebar does not scroll the page.

Max Bet: Sets the highest bet per line and all 10 lines by default. If Max Bet uses available credits is enabled, it keeps your current lines and denomination, ignores the Bet Per Line dropdown, and spins all available credits as one custom wager.

Definition: Bernoulli Trial

A Bernoulli trial is a random experiment with exactly two possible outcomes: success and failure.

The probability of success, p, stays constant for each independent trial. It models binary events like coin flips, pass/fail tests, purchases, and slot win/loss outcomes.
