# Sorry! — Slides + Safety (2 Players, 4 Pawns)

This version adds:
- **Slides**: Land on the start of a slide **that isn’t your color** and you slide forward to the end, bumping any pawns on the slide cells (including the end). Landing on your own color’s slide start does **not** slide.
- **Safety zone**: The last 5 cells (55–59) are a shared Safety area near Home; opponents **cannot land** on those cells. You still need an **exact count** to reach Home (60).
- **4 pawns per player**.

> Compact house-rules variant (not the full classic board geometry). If you want per-color safety zones and color-specific Home entries like the official board, I can extend this further.

## How to run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## Notes
- Cards supported: 1,2,3,4(back),5,7(split),8,10(10 or back 1),11(swap or 11),12, and SORRY.
- Enter from Start on 1, 2, or SORRY.
- You can’t land on your own pawn. Landing on an opponent bumps them to Start, except you **cannot land on** an opponent that’s in Safety.
